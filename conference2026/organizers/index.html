    Organizing committee

    The scverse conference 2026 is organized by members of the scverse community, together with our local partners at DTU, Copenhagen. scverse is a community-developed, non-profit project, fiscally sponsored by NumFOCUS. The conference is run by the people who build and maintain the ecosystem.

    Get involved

    The conference is a community effort. If you would like to help organise, volunteer during the event, or host a satellite session, reach out to us at conference2026@scverse.org.

    You can also learn more about the people behind scverse on the People page and about the project on our About page.
