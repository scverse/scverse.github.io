Community Calendar

Here’s an overview of what’s happening across the scverse community: our regular open community meetings and upcoming events such as hackathons and conferences. The calendar below stays in sync with our Google Calendar, so it’s always up to date. You can also subscribe to it and follow along from your own calendar app.

Book a meeting

The spatialdata developer sync is a bookable, biweekly meeting that runs only if someone books it — grab an open slot at least 24 hours ahead and you’ll get a calendar invite with the video-call link. It’s a developer-focused space to discuss ongoing work and onboard new contributors; no prior contribution needed. Wrong time zone? Reach out in #spatialdata-dev on Zulip.

Subscribe to the calendar

Add the scverse community calendar to your own calendar to get all our meetings and events automatically:

  • Add to Google Calendar
  • Subscribe via iCal (Apple Calendar, Outlook, Thunderbird, and other apps)

Open community meeting

Scverse community meetings happen every two weeks on Tuesday at 6pm CET and are open to everyone! If you are new to scverse, these meetings are a great way to get to know the people behind the project.

Agenda, next dates, meeting notes and the Zoom link are available from the meeting document. See our events page for past and upcoming hackathons and conferences.
