## Registration

Registration for scverse conference 2026 has closed. If you have a question about an existing registration, email [conference2026@scverse.org](mailto:conference2026@scverse.org).

Sign up for announcements via [Zulip](https://scverse.zulipchat.com/) or follow us on social media for the latest updates.

## Travel grants

We are committed to making the conference accessible to as many people as possible. As in previous years, we offered **travel grants** to help cover registration, accommodation, and travel.

Priority is given to applicants who have submitted an abstract and are in financial need — particularly those from underrepresented backgrounds, underresourced institutions, or low- and middle-income countries. Travel-grant applications closed on **August 20, 2026**, and award notifications were sent on **September 3, 2026**.

## Childcare & accessibility

We aim to support attendees with caregiving responsibilities and accessibility needs. If you have specific requirements, please reach out to [conference2026@scverse.org](mailto:conference2026@scverse.org) and we will do our best to accommodate.
